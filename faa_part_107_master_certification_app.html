Core Part 107 Knowledge Base

Essential ground school modules with instant rule breakdowns.

1

Regulations & Night (§ 107.29)

  • Alcohol Limit: 8 hours bottle-to-throttle, maximum 0.04% BAC.
  • Accident Reporting: Must report to FAA within 10 days if serious injury occurs (loss of consciousness/hospitalization) OR property damage exceeds $500.
  • Structure Height Exemption: May fly up to 400 ft above a structure if remaining within a 400 ft radius of that structure.
  • Right of Way: Drones must yield right-of-way to ALL manned aircraft at all times.
2

Airspace & Chart Reading

  • Class B: Solid Blue lines. Requires LAANC/ATC authorization. Big airport cake.
  • Class C: Solid Magenta lines. 2-tier shelf system. Requires LAANC/ATC authorization.
  • Class D: Dashed Blue cylinder surrounding small towered fields. Requires LAANC/ATC authorization.
  • Class G: Uncontrolled airspace. Surface to 700 or 1,200 ft AGL. No clearance needed.
  • CTAF (Common Traffic Advisory Frequency): Used at non-towered airports to broadcast position (usually 122.8 MHz).
3

Weather Data & METARs

  • Density Altitude: Pressure altitude corrected for non-standard temperature. High temperature + high humidity = High Density Altitude = REDUCED drone lift performance.
  • Stable Air: Stratiform clouds, smooth air, fair-to-poor visibility, continuous precipitation.
  • Unstable Air: Cumuliform clouds, turbulent air, good visibility, showery precipitation.
  • Dew Point Spread: Small temperature/dew point spread (< 3°C) indicates high likelihood of fog or low clouds.
4

Loading & Crew Resource Management

  • Load Factor in Turns: A 60° banked turn doubles the load factor ($2.0 \text{ G}$). Stalling speed increases dramatically.
  • CG Shift: Forward CG increases stability but reduces performance. Aft CG makes aircraft unstable and difficult to control.
  • 5 Hazardous Attitudes & Antidotes:
    • Anti-authority ("Don't tell me") → Follow the rules.
    • Impulsivity ("Do it quickly") → Not so fast. Think first.
    • Invulnerability ("It won't happen to me") → It could happen to me.
    • Macho ("I can do it") → Taking chances is foolish.
    • Resignation ("What's the use?") → I am not helpless.

Advanced Sector Track: Agriculture & Part 137 Spraying

Regulatory workflows and mission planning for spraying, crop analytics, and heavy payloads (>55 lbs).

Part 107 (Crop Scouting & Monitoring)

You can fly multispectral sensors, standard cameras, and RGB sensors to map health (NDVI) under basic Part 107 rules. No special agricultural certificate is required if you are not dispensing any material.

Basic 107 Remote Pilot License Sufficient

Part 137 (Dispensing Economic Poisons)

Applying pesticides, fertilizers, seeds, or chemical liquids requires an FAA Part 137 Agricultural Aircraft Operator Certificate. If your drone weighs over 55 lbs (e.g., DJI Agras T40/T50 loaded), you also need an FAA § 44807 Exemption and a Class 3 Medical Certificate.

Part 137 Cert + § 44807 Heavy Drone Exemption Required

Advanced Sector Track: Night Operations (§ 107.29)

Requirements for night flying without waivers, night physiology, and anti-collision lighting specs.

Night Physiology & Optical Illusions

  • Rods vs Cones: Cones detect color and detail in bright light (fovea center). Rods detect movement and operate at night (off-center scanning).
  • Dark Adaptation: Takes 30 minutes for eyes to fully adjust to darkness. Use low-intensity red flashlights to preserve night vision.
  • Autokinesis: Staring at a stationary single point of light in the dark causes it to appear to move rapidly, causing control loss.

§ 107.29 Anti-Collision Lighting Hardware Checklist

Visibility Distance: Minimum 3 Statute Miles (SM)
Flash Rate Requirement: 40 to 100 Flashes Per Minute
Mounting Position: Top & Bottom for 360° Sphere Coverage

Note on LAANC at Night: You can request night LAANC authorization in controlled airspace (Class B, C, D, E surface) up to the published grid altitude ceiling.
